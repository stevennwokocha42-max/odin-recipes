Jay's Signature Pizza Crust

Description

This pizza crust recipe is a classic and versatile base for any pizza creation. It's easy to make and yields a deliciously crispy and chewy crust that pairs perfectly with your favorite toppings.

Whether you're a seasoned pizza maker or a beginner, this recipe will help you create a homemade pizza that rivals your favorite pizzeria.

Ingredients

  • 1 ½ cups warm water (110 degrees F/45 degrees C)
  • 2 ¼ teaspoons active dry yeast
  • 3 ½ cups all-purpose flour, divided
  • 2 tablespoons olive oil
  • ½ teaspoons brown sugar
  • 1 teaspoon salt

Steps

  1. Stir together warm water, yeast, and brown sugar in a large mixing bowl; let sit for 10 minutes.
  2. Stir in 2 cups of flour, olive oil, and salt. Gradually add remaining flour until dough is no longer sticky.
  3. Turn dough out onto a floured surface and knead until smooth and elastic, about 8 minutes.
  4. Place dough in a greased bowl, cover, and let rise in a warm place until doubled in size, about 1 hour.
  5. Preheat oven to 475 degrees F (245 degrees C).
  6. Roll dough out onto a greased pizza pan or baking sheet. Add your favorite toppings.
  7. Bake in the preheated oven until crust is golden brown and cheese is bubbly, about 12-15 minutes.
  8. Remove from oven and let cool for a few minutes before slicing and serving.
